The evening

Organized and produced by Mirla Criste Agnir (Oberlin ’85), Night at the Cat brought alumni together for an evening of music, poetry, and creativity. This archive celebrates the featured artists, guest collaborators, and the work they continue to create.

Performance recordings will be added as authorized video becomes available. Visit our Night at the Cat YouTube channel.

Sharing photos or video

Have reunion photographs or a performance recording to share? Use our private Dropbox upload request. You do not need a Dropbox account to send files.

Upload Photos or Videos

Our Dropbox storage is limited, so for large performance videos or collections of photos, upload them to your own Google Drive or another file-sharing service and email archive@nightatthecat.org a link with download access. Your files do not need to be publicly searchable.

Please identify yourself, the performance or people shown, and relevant credits. Email us to let us know whether we may publish or embed your materials. Sending or uploading a file does not itself grant publication permission; we will confirm before posting.
